The controller reports as **`DDJ-REV1 (2b73:003f)`**. It matches `isDJDevice()` on the

## Step 3 — three bands

Under a full mix at set level: bass ≈ 0.59–0.64, mid ≈ 0.46–0.58, high ≈ 0.16–0.33,
all moving independently. With the stream live and no music, all three read exactly `0`
— the "no `Math.sin` fallback" rule at `CONTRIBUTING.md:151` is holding.

## Step 4 — band isolation on the real signal chain

Measured against the same assertion `test/verify-audio.mjs:123-124` makes: the expected
band must clear `0.05` and exceed the next loudest by `2.5×`.

```
bass solo →  bass 0.274   mid 0.116   high 0.031    ratio 2.36    FAIL (need 2.5)
hats solo →  bass 0.000   mid 0.174   high 0.253    ratio 1.46    FAIL (need 2.5)
```

The threshold was fixed before the run and has not been moved after seeing the numbers.
**As specified, this step fails.**

The evidence nevertheless shows the band mapping is correct, via cross-condition
comparison:

| Band | Bass solo | Hats solo | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| bass | 0.274 | **0.000** | falls to a hard zero |
| high | 0.031 | 0.253 | rises **8.2×** |

Killing the bass channel zeroes the bass meter; soloing hats raises high eight-fold.
That is not a wiring or channel-mapping fault. What fails is the *test*: `2.5×`
within-condition dominance was calibrated on pure sine tones, where a 100 Hz input has
almost no mid energy. Real music routed through a "bass only" channel carries harmonics
into mid, and hats carry substantial mid. **Step 4 as written cannot pass against a real
mixer regardless of how correct the app is.** See finding F2.

## Step 5 — BPM

Settled **308 ms** after audio start. Held **121 with zero spread** through Particles and
Rings.

Controlled load test, single track, alternating light and heavy modes:

```
light  (particles)  median 122   spread 24
heavy  (polygons)   median 124   spread 22
heavy  (galaxy)     median 125   spread 24
light  (particles)  median 127   spread 26
```

**Render load does not move BPM.** The 2–3 BPM drift continues upward through the return
to a light mode, so it tracks elapsed time rather than load. `audioProcessor.kickNode`
remained a live `AudioWorkletNode` throughout. The claim that detection survives a heavy
render loop is verified against real hardware.

Spread of 22–26 BPM in *every* window, light and heavy alike, is a separate concern —
finding F3.

An earlier uncontrolled pass showed medians shifting across modes (121→132). That is
**not** evidence of load starvation: the track changed mid-session, confounding mode with
tempo. It is recorded here only so the number is not mistaken for a finding later.

## Findings — each needs its own ticket

**F1 · Spectrum Bars fails at projection resolution, and the docs recommend it.**
p50 25.1 / p05 21.1 fullscreen at 1920×1280, against 59.9/51.3 on the laptop panel. It is
fill-rate bound. `CONTRIBUTING.md:127` names Spectrum and Particles as the two safe modes
on a slower machine — at projection size Spectrum is the *worst* of the ten and Particles
holds 60. The documented advice is backwards for the one context that matters.

**F2 · Step 4's threshold is unpassable against a real mixer.**
`2.5×` within-condition dominance is a sine-tone criterion applied to broadband material.
Replace with the cross-condition delta actually measured here (bass 0.274 → 0.000; high
0.031 → 0.253), which uses both windows and would still catch a genuine mis-mapping.

**F3 · BPM readout excursions of ~20 BPM on real material.**
Median ~122 with maxima of 143–145 in every window, independent of render load. Not
starvation — the worklet is confirmed active — but the readout is noisier than "settles
and holds" implies.

**F4 · Virtual audio devices are treated as DJ hardware — OBSERVED, not hypothetical.**
`Serato Virtual Audio (Virtual)` matches `isDJDevice()` on the `serato` keyword and sorts
above the built-in microphone. This was noted early in the run as a *plausible* failure
while the real controller was present.

It then happened. Later in the session the DDJ-REV1 dropped off the device list — asleep,
powered down, or unplugged. A page loaded at that moment auto-selected
`Serato Virtual Audio (Virtual)`, presented it as **`DJ · Serato Virtual Audio (Virtual)`**,
and reported **`Ready:`**. No warning, no fallback to the microphone, no indication the
source was a silent loopback.

This is the most dangerous finding of the run, because unlike F1 it fails *quietly*: at
load-in the operator sees `DJ ·` and `Ready`, presses Start, and then debugs a dead
visualizer by looking at the mixer. Two parts to fixing it:

* A device matched only by a virtual/loopback name must not outrank a physical input, and
  a virtual device must never carry the `DJ ·` prefix.
* `Ready:` should not claim readiness for a source producing no signal. A
  "selected but silent" state is exactly the condition the no-`Math.sin`-fallback rule at
  `CONTRIBUTING.md:151` exists to keep visible, and the device rail currently hides it.

**F5 · Deep single-frame stalls in Polygon Collage and Mandala.**
Minima of 10.6 and 15.1 FPS against p05 values of 37.0 and 57.1. Passes the percentile
floor while dropping frames visibly.

**F6 · The projector itself is still unchecked.**
External display is not a projector. Throw distance, room brightness and rail legibility
at real viewing distance need a Pennovation site visit before Founders Alley.

**F7 · Sustained thermal behaviour untested.**
Named in the ticket's background but not one of the eight steps, and twenty minutes
cannot measure it. Needs a full-set soak.

## Notes on the instrument

`test/hardware-probe.js` carries four fixes that each produced a *wrong answer* before
being found. They are documented in the file header because they will bite the next run
otherwise:

1. **Focus.** Chrome throttles rAF and timers in a window that is not frontmost. Measured
   that way Spectrum reads 25 FPS on a machine doing 60 — the same number as the genuine
   F1 failure, arrived at for an entirely different reason. Every frame now records
   `document.hasFocus()`, and unfocused frames are excluded rather than averaged in.
2. **Persistence.** The first session lived in a page variable and a stray reload erased
   it. Every mark now snapshots to `localStorage`; `hw.restore()` recovers a run.
3. **Key dispatch.** `app.js:50` switches on `e.code` (`Digit5`), not `e.key`, and the
   guard at `app.js:44` calls `e.target.matches()`, which throws when the event is
   dispatched at `document`. Synthetic keys must go to `document.body` with a `code`.
4. **Self-pacing.** A countdown the operator has to catch is a countdown the operator
   misses while walking to the mixer. Steps needing hands wait for a keypress, with a
   live band readout on screen so isolation can be confirmed before the hold starts.

A driver note: CDP `Runtime.evaluate` times out at 45 s. Keep any single blocking
measurement under ~40 s, or fire it and poll.
